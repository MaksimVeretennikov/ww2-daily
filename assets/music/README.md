# Royalty-free music for the short videos

Drop a few **royalty-free / CC0** tracks here as `.mp3` (or `.m4a`). The video
generator picks one at random per clip and lays it under the voiceover at low
volume.

Use only music you're allowed to use on YouTube/VK/etc. Good free sources:
- YouTube Audio Library (filter: no attribution required)
- Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music) — CC0-like license
- Free Music Archive (CC0 / public domain)

Pick calm, neutral, slightly solemn instrumental tracks that fit archival war
footage. No files here = videos are built without music (voice + text only).
